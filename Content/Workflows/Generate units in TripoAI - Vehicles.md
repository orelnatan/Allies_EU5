
## Generate multiview images

- Use GPT/Gemini/Clode etc to create prompts
- Generate multiview images in TripoAI

## Generate 3D model in TripoAI

- Click "Multiview to 3D", and under "Model" tab, click "HD Model".
- Under "Geometry & Texture": Texture - 4K, Triangle - 12,000.
- Leave all the rest as it is, Hit "Generate".
